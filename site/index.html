Compatibility & important details +

Visuals and animations illustrate workflows, not a live connection. LinkSphere provides device tools; your connected AI assistant plans tasks.

Desktop actions support Windows, macOS and Android after local setup. Native Linux desktop control is not available. Android screen actions require a paired accessibility companion or an unlocked device with root/ADB access.

P2P depends on network conditions; relay is used when unavailable. Web uploads have a 1 GiB limit.
